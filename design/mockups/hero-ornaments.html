Hero ornaments

The baseline is today's hero, then four directions taken from a real object or a real light. Each one shows the hero, plus Contact and Stack, to see how far it carries.

Since the arcs, glow and radar left, the cards only differ by their text.

APendant lamp over the headline

A tiered pendant hangs from the hero's top edge and lights the headline like a table. It brings back the warm glow you liked, but now it has a source. Contact reuses the family as a small wall sconce; Stack stays plain, so the light keeps a single role.

BGrooved tile band

A column of clay tiles with U grooves is set into the hero's right edge, like a tiled wall next to the text. The tile changes with the ambiance: sage glaze, terracotta, brown clay, oxblood. Contact gets a thin strip of the same tile as a skirting. It's a material, not a light.

CSunlight through a breeze block screen

Afternoon sun crosses a perforated concrete screen and lands on the hero as soft shapes. It's pure light, with no object drawn. The screen comes from Palm Springs houses of the 1950s, outside our folder, but the plaster rooms in sunlight (06 to 08) are the same mood. Stack gets a smaller patch.

DAmber glass blocks

A small window of amber glass blocks in the hero's corner, lit from behind, spills a warm tint on the card. Light and object in one, smaller than the lamp. Contact uses a single block as its mark.
